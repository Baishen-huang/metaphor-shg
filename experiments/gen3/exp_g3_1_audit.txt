【表 7】裁决规则与结果
  规则（预先声明，不事后调参）：
    dead      CI 覆盖 0.5 且 |配对AUC−0.5| < 0.02
    harmful   配对 AUC < 0.5 且 CI 上界 < 0.5（显著反向）
    useful    CI 下界 > 0.5 且 |配对AUC−0.5| ≥ 0.02（显著且非平凡）
    weak      CI 下界 > 0.5 但 |配对AUC−0.5| < 0.02（显著但幅度小）
    redundant 与某一维的秩相关 ≥ 0.8 或二值重叠 Jaccard ≥ 0.8
    sem              配对AUC=0.6277 [0.6023,0.6522] → useful+redundant(struct)+redundant(type)
    struct           配对AUC=0.5197 [0.5063,0.5323] → weak+redundant(sem)+redundant(type)
    clue             配对AUC=0.5087 [0.5043,0.5132] → weak+redundant(same_frame)+redundant(same_cascade)+redundant(ground_jaccard)
    type             配对AUC=0.5249 [0.5092,0.5401] → useful+redundant(sem)+redundant(struct)
    same_frame       配对AUC=0.5053 [0.5023,0.5087] → weak+redundant(clue)+redundant(same_cascade)+redundant(ground_jaccard)
    same_cascade     配对AUC=0.5070 [0.5030,0.5116] → weak+redundant(clue)+redundant(same_frame)+redundant(ground_jaccard)
    ground_jaccard   配对AUC=0.5300 [0.5215,0.5392] → useful+redundant(clue)+redundant(same_frame)+redundant(same_cascade)
